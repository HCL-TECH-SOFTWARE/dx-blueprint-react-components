# Content Types

Content Types are also known as **Content Templates** in HCL DX.
</header>

**Content types** or **content templates** are predefined structures or formats for a piece of content in HCL DX that help to ensure consistency and standardization in content creation, organization, and presentation. They make **managing** and **publishing** content across different channels and platforms easier. Blueprint page components make use of these content templates to understand your content and how to present them. You can [create your own content templates](https://help.hcl-software.com/digital-experience/9.5/latest/manage_content/wcm_authoring/content_composer/usage/author_and_manage_content_templates/author_content_templates/) to suit your specific needs, or you can use the ones that are provided by the Blueprint design system.

HCL Blueprint comes with the following content templates:

1. Ad - Create visually appealing and concise advertising content for promotional campaigns.
2. Banner - Design prominent banners to capture attention and convey key messages across the digital experience.
3. Banner Stripe - Generate sleek, narrow banner elements for subtle yet impactful messaging.
4. Business Spotlight - Showcase specific businesses or initiatives with dedicated, informative content.
5. Countdown - Implement dynamic countdown timers for events, promotions, or deadlines.
6. Data Table - Structure and present tabular data clearly and efficiently for easy readability.
7. Department - Create dedicated content sections for specific departments, outlining their services and information.
8. Events - Publish and manage detailed event listings with all necessary information for attendees.
9. Expense Card - Develop content for displaying individual expense details in a standardized card format.
10. Gallery - Curate and display collections of images or videos in an engaging, browsable format.
11. Hero Banner - Craft large, impactful hero sections for main page introductions and critical announcements.
12. News - Distribute timely news articles and updates to keep your audience informed.
13. Onboarding - This content template is designed for creating a reusable component that references other content elements by allowing you to combine Text, HTML, and Option Selection elements.
14. Portal Alert - Issue urgent or important notifications directly within the DX page.
15. Product - Detail product features, specifications, and benefits for e-commerce or informational pages.
16. Questions - Formulate interactive question-and-answer sections or FAQs for user engagement.
17. Quick Link - Create easily accessible shortcut links to important pages or resources.
18. Resource - Organize and present various helpful resources, such as documents, guides, or external links.
19. Search suggestions - Configure and manage suggested search terms to enhance user search experience.
20. Video - Embed and manage video content for dynamic and engaging presentations.
